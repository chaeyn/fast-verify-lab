# Code of conduct

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. It applies to maintainers and contributors.

## Expected conduct

Treat other participants with respect. Discuss the code or proposal. Give specific feedback and evidence. Accept that people have different experience, languages, and access needs.

Use welcoming language. Correct mistakes without personal attacks. Respect another person's request to end a conversation.

## Unacceptable conduct

Do not harass, threaten, insult, or discriminate against participants. Do not post sexual content, private information, credentials, or repeated unwanted messages.

Do not use project spaces to target a person or group. Do not disrupt a discussion after a maintainer asks you to stop.

## Report a problem

For routine moderation, contact `@chaeyn` on the relevant GitHub discussion. Keep personal details out of public comments.

For a confidential report, use a private contact method listed on the [maintainer profile](https://github.com/chaeyn). Do not use a public issue to disclose private evidence.

Describe the event, location, and requested response. Share only information that you can disclose. The maintainer will limit report details to people needed for a response.

## Enforcement

The maintainer can edit or remove content, close discussions, issue a warning, or block participation. The response depends on the conduct and its impact.

The maintainer can act without a prior warning when conduct threatens safety or exposes private information. Reporters and participants can request a review of a decision through the same contact route.
